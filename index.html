Questions before you book

What if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight, so your pick-up follows your landing time. A delay does not change your fixed fare.

Is the airport barrier fee included?

Yes. Your fixed fare includes the airport barrier fee.

How long will my driver wait?

45 minutes after landing are included. After that, waiting is 35p a minute.

Where will my driver meet me?

Your booking confirmation tells you exactly where to meet, and you can message dispatch on WhatsApp at any time.

How do I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, or by card online through Stripe.

Can you take large groups?

Yes. 8-seaters and minibuses carry up to 16 passengers with their luggage.
